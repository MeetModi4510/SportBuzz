19:02 Local Time, 18:02 GMT, 23:32 IST: It was an absorbing and eventful day of Test cricket, with momentum swinging at different stages. England enjoyed a productive opening session, picking up a couple of wickets, but New Zealand ensured they kept the scoreboard moving with some valuable runs. As the day progressed, however, the visitors gradually gained the upper hand. Although wickets fell at regular intervals, New Zealand's middle order responded admirably. Daryl Mitchell, Tom Blundell and Glenn Phillips all made important contributions, stitching together useful partnerships and keeping the innings on track through the latter half of the day. Their efforts allowed New Zealand to build a solid platform despite England's breakthroughs. For the hosts, Sonny Baker and Jacob Bethell were the standout performers with the ball, claiming two wickets apiece and ensuring England remained in the contest.
